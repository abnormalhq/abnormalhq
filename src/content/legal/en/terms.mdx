## The service

- Abnormal ("we", "us") provides @abnormalhq.com mailboxes and continues to support existing @abnormal.bid mailboxes. You create a shop account, buy a plan in the shop, receive a redeem code once you pay, pick your own username and password in My account or on the redeem page, and then read and send mail in webmail or any IMAP / SMTP mail app.
- Mail is sent, received and stored by Stalwart mail servers that we set up and run ourselves.
- Team plans can be purchased per seat in the shop and support an own domain as described by the plan. Needs beyond the listed plans and setup help can be discussed as custom services.
- Each plan's number of mailboxes, available domains, storage per mailbox, service period, redemption window and price are as shown on the shop page when you order.

## Buying and paying

- You can pay with Linux DO Credit (LDC), by card through Stripe, or with USDT on the TRON network (TRC-20); the methods available are those shown in the shop. The price is calculated by our server when you order and saved with the order, so later price changes don't affect orders already created.
- An order is placed once the payment provider confirms the payment. The redeem code is then issued automatically. If it doesn't appear for a long time after you pay, click "Refresh payment result" on the order page or contact us.
- You collect the redeem code in the same browser, or by signing in to My account. Keep it safe: anyone who has it can open mailboxes with it, and losses caused by sharing or leaking your code are your responsibility.
- Card details are handled through Stripe’s secure payment form, embedded on our website or opened on a Stripe page. Any fees a payment provider charges follow its own rules.
- When paying with USDT, send the exact amount shown on the order page to the address that belongs to that order, and send USDT on the TRON network (TRC-20) only: payments sent on another network, in another token or to another address are lost and we can't recover them. Exchange withdrawal and wallet fees are yours to bear, and the amount that arrives must be at least the order amount; the order is placed once the total confirmed on chain reaches it. Transfers on a blockchain are public: anyone can see what the payment address received.

## Redeeming and opening mailboxes

- A redeem code must be used within the redemption window set by the plan, after which it expires. How many mailboxes a code can open and which domains it allows are fixed when it is issued.
- In My account you can set up mailboxes with the codes of your own orders, and reset their passwords, disable them or delete them. Deleting a mailbox erases its mail for good and cannot be undone; its slot goes back to the order.
- Mailboxes set up with the codes of your orders, including ones someone else set up with a code you gave them, are managed by you in My account. Tell people this before you give them a code.
- Usernames are first come, first served and must follow the rules on the redeem page. Reserved names, names already taken, and misleading names (for example ones that impersonate other people, organisations or us) can't be used, and we may reclaim such names.
- The password in the redeem result is shown only once, so save it right away. You can change it yourself in webmail settings.
- If a plan's storage is 0 or not stated, the server's default quota applies; that doesn't mean unlimited storage. Each mailbox also has a daily sending limit to prevent abuse.

## Service period, renewal and expiry

- **Free trial and upgrades**: each verified account can claim one free 7-day trial with one 1 GB mailbox, without a card or automatic charge. The trial is suspended at expiry with no grace period, and mail is retained for 30 days. During retention you can manually upgrade to a single-mailbox or Team plan, keeping your address, password and mail. The existing mailbox counts toward the Team seat total. Paid service starts when payment is confirmed. Both LDC and card trial upgrades are one-time payments and require manual renewal afterward. An eligible WELCOME10 code takes 10% off the first paid period only; later periods use the regular plan price, and the code’s expiry and usage limits apply.
- **Service period**: for subscription plans, the service period starts when payment succeeds, not when you redeem, so redeem soon after paying. The length depends on the plan. Plans without a service period are not subject to the renewal and expiry rules in this section.
- **Renewal**: LDC plans can be renewed before expiry through the link in the reminder email or the order page, and the new period continues from the original expiry date; subscriptions bought with USDT are renewed the same way, by hand and with USDT. Subscriptions purchased directly by card are renewed automatically by Stripe each period. You can cancel automatic renewal or change your card on My account at any time; after cancelling, the service ends at the close of the current period.
- **Grace period**: for 7 days after a paid subscription expires the mailbox keeps sending and receiving as usual, and renewing during this time continues from the original expiry date.
- **Suspension and retention**: when the grace period ends, the mailbox is suspended and can no longer be used to sign in or send, but its mail and data are kept and new mail is still received. Renewing within 30 days of suspension starts a new period from the time of payment and restores the mailbox right away.
- **Reclaiming**: if the mailbox is still not renewed when retention ends, it is marked for reclaiming and we may delete it with all its mail. Deleted mail can't be recovered, and the username may later be taken by someone else. Back up any mail you need before expiry.
- We send expiry and renewal reminders to the contact email you gave when ordering, or to the subscription's mailboxes if you didn't give one. Reminders are a courtesy; not receiving one doesn't change these periods.

## Cancellation and refunds

- **Custom services**: full refund before the service starts; after that, as set out in the confirmed plan.
- **USDT payments**: a confirmed blockchain transfer can't be reversed and there are no chargebacks, so a refund of a USDT order is a separate transfer from us, as follows:
  - **Refund address**: contact us from the contact email you gave when ordering, or from your signed-in shop account, with the order number and a TRON address that can receive TRC-20 USDT. We only refund to an address you give us in writing, not to the address the payment came from (when you withdraw from an exchange, that address usually belongs to the exchange). Checking the address is your responsibility; we can't recover a refund sent to a wrong address you gave.
  - **Amount**: refunds are made in USDT for the same number of USDT, with no exchange-rate conversion. When the refund is our fault (for example, a long outage, or we can't deliver), we pay the network fee of the refund transfer. Otherwise (for example, an unused code, an overpayment, or a payment that can no longer be delivered at its price) that network fee is deducted from the refund, and we tell you the amount before sending. An amount smaller than the network fee can't be refunded.
  - **Over- and underpayment**: you can ask for anything paid above the order amount back. If less than the order amount arrives, the order isn't placed: you can send the rest to complete it, or ask for what arrived back.
  - **Payments that can't be delivered**: if a payment arrives when the order can no longer be delivered at its price (for example, its discount code's hold lapsed and the code's last use was taken, or the subscription was renewed after added seats were priced), no service is delivered and you can ask for a refund.
  - **What can't be refunded**: payments sent on another network, in another token or to another address never reach the order's address, and we can't refund them.
  - **Timing**: we send the refund within 7 business days of receiving a valid refund address, and give you the transaction hash.
- No refund is given for mailboxes suspended for breaking "Acceptable use". Refunds go back the way you paid: LDC through Linux DO Credit and cards through Stripe, with timing depending on the provider; USDT by transfer as set out above.
